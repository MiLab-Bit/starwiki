# 主题曲定稿 · The Road Home / 星河有归

## 标题互补

| 英文 | 中文 |
|---|---|
| **The Road Home** | 强调旅程、寻找、回归 |
| **星河有归** | 强调宇宙万物皆有归处 |

英文保留演唱版本；中文为意境对应翻译，非生硬直译。

## 歌词（双语最终版）

### Verse 1

Beneath the silence of the universe  
Where ancient starlights sleep  

在宇宙寂静的深处，  
古老的星光沉沉安眠。

A lonely signal drifts beyond  
The oceans of the deep  

一道孤独的讯号漂向远方，  
穿越深邃无垠的海洋。

Through endless tides of time and space  
Through shadows of the night  

穿越时间与空间的无尽潮汐，  
穿过漫漫长夜的幽影。

A thousand fading stars remember  
A distant hidden light  

千万颗正在消逝的星辰，  
仍记得那遥远而隐秘的光。

### Pre-Chorus

If fate is but a broken compass  
Lost beneath the sky  

若命运不过是一枚破碎的罗盘，  
遗落在苍穹之下。

Let the winds of endless ages  
Carry echoes home tonight  

便让无尽岁月的风，  
带着古老的回响，于今夜归来。

### Chorus

At the edge of the universe  
Where the last stars fall  

在宇宙的边缘，  
最后的星辰正在坠落。

A thousand worlds, a thousand dreams  
Still answer to the call  

千万世界，千万梦想，  
仍回应着那古老的召唤。

Through silver skies and oceans blue  
Through every road unknown  

穿越银色的天空，  
穿越蔚蓝的海洋，  
穿过所有未知的道路。

All paths beneath the heavens  
Will lead the lost back home  

苍穹之下所有的道路，  
终将引领迷途之灵回归故乡。

Eternity is not the endless time  
Nor words forever carved in stone  

永恒并非无尽流逝的时间，  
也不是刻在石上的不朽誓言。

It is the silent light beyond the stars  
That calls all wandering souls back home  

它是群星之外那束寂静的光，  
呼唤所有漂泊的灵魂回归。

### Verse 2

There were seas without a shoreline  
There were kingdoms made of dust  

曾有无岸之海，  
曾有尘埃筑成的王国。

There were names beneath the ages  
Lost to time and rust  

曾有名字埋藏于漫长岁月，  
被时间与锈蚀遗忘。

The heavens kept their ancient songs  
The galaxies their flame  

苍穹保存着古老的歌谣，  
银河守护着不灭的火焰。

Until the last wandering comet  
Returned unto its name  

直到最后一颗流浪的彗星，  
回归它曾属于的名字。

### Pre-Chorus 2

Perhaps the stars were written  
Before the dawn of time  

也许星辰早已被写下，  
在黎明与时间诞生之前。

Perhaps each broken fragment  
Was searching to align  

也许每一片破碎的碎片，  
都在寻找彼此的契合。

### Bridge

When the final moon is sleeping  
When the ancient worlds are gone  

当最后的月亮沉睡，  
当远古的世界消亡。

I will follow every signal  
Until the last light carries on  

我仍会追随每一道讯号，  
直到最后的光芒继续燃烧。

Beyond the veil of endless skies  
Beyond the fading years  

越过无尽天空的帷幕，  
越过逐渐消逝的岁月。

The stars will sing the forgotten song  
That once brought us back here  

群星将再次唱起那被遗忘的歌，  
那首曾带我们回到此处的歌。

### Final Chorus

At the edge of the universe  
Where all horizons fade  

在宇宙的尽头，  
当所有地平线渐渐消融。

The stars remember ancient vows  
The paths the cosmos made  

群星仍记得古老的誓言，  
记得宇宙曾留下的道路。

Through every world that ever was  
Through every dream once known  

穿越曾存在过的每个世界，  
穿越每一个曾被知晓的梦境。

All rivers flowing through the heavens  
Will find their way back home  

流经天穹的万千河流，  
终将寻回自己的归途。

### Outro

Not a place  
Not a time  

不是一个地方，  
不是一个时代。

Only the light  
That knows the way home  

唯有那束光，  
仍知归途。
